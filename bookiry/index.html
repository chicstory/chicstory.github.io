Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you store files in my Google Drive instead of your database?

Your thoughts are deeply personal. We believe reading notes should never be locked behind a startup's private database. By storing notes in your own Google Drive (Bookiry/ folder), you retain 100% ownership forever—even if Bookiry disappeared tomorrow.

How does the Obsidian sync work?

When you open any book, Bookiry pre-generates a clean 00_Index.md with standard YAML frontmatter, your 4 catalytic sparks, and your appended reflection timestamps. You can copy it or direct-sync to your Obsidian vault seamlessly.

What is the difference between Monthly ($3.90) and Annual ($39)?

Both plans unlock all 4 catalytic sparks, Google Drive 2-way sync, and Obsidian export. The Monthly Plan ($3.90/mo) offers ultimate flexibility with up to 30 books analyzed each month. The Annual Plan ($39/yr) gives you 2 months free (saving 17%) and expands your quota to 50 books per month.

Can I request a refund if it is not for me?

Absolutely. If Bookiry doesn't bring peace and deep resonance to your reading within 14 days, just drop us an email for a full, courteous 100% refund.
