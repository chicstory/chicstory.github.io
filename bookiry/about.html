About Bookiry

Why we curate catalytic questions instead of 5-minute book summaries.

🧭 The Solitary Dilemma of Modern Reading

We live in an era flooded with synthesized summaries, AI cheat-sheets, and bite-sized book recaps. Yet most readers confess to a quiet paradox: you finish reading a brilliant book, feel enlightened for forty-eight hours, and three weeks later, you can scarcely recall its central conviction.

Summaries inform the cognitive mind, but they rarely transform personal character. Real intellectual transformation does not stem from collecting pre-chewed conclusions—it is sparked by the specific, resonant questions you carry while turning each page.

"A prudent question is one-half of wisdom." — Francis Bacon

✨ The 4 Catalytic Sparks (Socratic Quad)

Before opening page one, Bookiry invites you to calibrate your internal reading compass. Whether your current season calls for Rest & Resonance, Work & Growth, Curiosity & Fiction, or a Custom Personal Quest, Bookiry crafts four intentional inquiries:

1. Spark: A perspective-shifting inquiry before turning the first page.
2. Lens: An exact emotional or cognitive tension to observe in the text.
3. Quest: A philosophical dilemma that challenges your everyday assumptions.
4. Echo: A lasting personal conviction you take back into your real life.

🔒 Zero Vendor Lock-in: 100% Private Ownership

Your reflections are deeply sacred. Bookiry rejects the conventional model of trapping reader notes inside a private corporate database.

When you connect your Google Account, Bookiry utilizes the restricted drive.file scope—meaning our application can only see and manage notes inside your own private Bookiry/ folder. We also support seamless markdown exports for Obsidian and local vaults. Even if Bookiry ceases to exist tomorrow, your reading sanctuary remains yours forever.

🌱 Sustainable & Free Public Utility

Bookiry is engineered as a lean, zero-cost public utility. By pairing Google's advanced lightweight intelligence with high-standard web typography, we offer deep inquiry generation free of charge to readers worldwide, supported non-intrusively through privacy-respecting digital advertising.
